Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Personal Content Scheduler ("the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect your TikTok account via TikTok OAuth2 and the Content Posting API.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the App, we only request the minimum TikTok API scopes necessary to operate:

  • Basic Profile Info (user.info.basic): Display name and account identifier used to confirm the connected TikTok account.
  • Content Upload & Publishing (video.upload / video.publish): Used strictly to upload or publish videos that you explicitly provide or schedule.

2. How We Use Information

Authentication tokens and video metadata are used solely to execute your requested video uploads and scheduling actions. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or TikTok tokens with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

OAuth2 access and refresh tokens are stored securely within your local or private automation environment and are transmitted only over encrypted HTTPS connections to TikTok's official API endpoints.

4. Data Retention & Revocation

You may revoke the App's access at any time from your TikTok account settings under Security & Permissions → Manage app permissions. Upon revocation or request, all locally stored tokens are deleted.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact the app administrator via the developer contact details registered on the TikTok Developer Portal.
